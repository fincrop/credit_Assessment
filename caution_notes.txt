The lease factor (0.35) — how much a cultivated-but-not-owned plot counts toward this borrower. That's a credit-risk stance; your lending partner may want it lower (or to exclude non-owned plots entirely). And the headline aggregation is tenure+area-weighted mean — some lenders prefer a deliberately conservative "weakest-plot-aware" number instead. Both are one-line changes in the aggregator; I'd just flag them so they're a conscious decision rather than a default that ships silently into a lending product.
